Meet the cell

The supporting cast

Each organelle has a specialized role. Together, they help the cell store information, build molecules, and stay organized.

01 · Information hub

Nucleus

The nucleus stores the cell’s DNA and helps regulate gene expression. Its instructions are copied into mRNA, which can travel out to a ribosome for translation.

02 · Synthesis & transport

Endoplasmic reticulum

The ER is a membrane network. Rough ER, studded with ribosomes, helps fold and process proteins destined for membranes or secretion; smooth ER makes lipids and has other roles.

03 · Protein builder

Ribosome

Ribosomes read mRNA codons and link amino acids into a polypeptide. They are essential to gene expression and can be free in the cytoplasm or attached to rough ER.
